# Mod reference sheet

---

## Two defaults

**DM unless noted.** Moving a correction out of channel is most of the work. It keeps the fix from becoming a scene, and it keeps the server from feeling supervised.

**Don't announce removals.** A quiet removal fits a server where nobody tracks participation. A public one makes the room feel watched.

---

## Personal or heavy content in a game channel

- "Hey, wrong room for this one — but I hope you're doing okay, seriously."
- "This isn't really a server that has a place for this, and I'm sorry, because it sounds like a lot. Not a strike against you, just nowhere here to put it."

The apology is really important here. This is the case where a flat correction can actually hurt someone. Other responses can stay flat.

### Whether to delete it

Usually yes, but the reason isn't the post itself, but rather what grows on it (heheh, like a mold). If the post is left there, people reply kindly, and then the Arc Raiders chat has a support thread in it. That puts everyone else in an awkward position. Ignore it and feel cold, or engage and they're doing the thing this server literally doesn't do.

Order of action that avoids making this insult on top of injury:

1. **DM first.** Use one of the lines above.
2. **Ask them to take it down,** with an out: "would you mind pulling it? or I can, whichever's easier." Probably most people are gonna wanna pull it down themselves and then that deletion wasn't done *to* them.
3. **Remove it ourselves** if they don't respond in a few hours, or if replies are already stacking. Tell the person that it was removed.

**Two exceptions:**

- **Replies already accumulated.** Deleting takes other people's responses with it or leaves them in an awkward void. Sometimes it's better to just let that one conversation finish and say nothing. Let it quietly die, if that's the move from here.
- **It looks like someone is in a no-kidding crisis.** The post is no longer the issue. See the "Crisis" section below. We no longer really give that much of a shit about the channel.

## Right server, wrong channel

Responding in-channel is fine for these ones.

- "Wrong channel, but I respect it. #farm-and-date 👉"
- "This one lives in #gather-items-for-mama"
- "→ #talking-about-gaming"

## Not about gaming, but light in tone

Memes, a set of cat pics... anything more than a one-off comment or post that we think will die right away. If there's nowhere to go, then they need to go back to their home servers? heh

- "Ha, nice - no home for this one here though."
- "Cuteeee!! Nowhere to put it, unfortunately"
- "No channel for this one, fam"

**Let one-offs go.** A single stray post is not the social layer inherently arriving upon our dearest server, and commenting on it honestly sometimes costs more than ignoring it. But if it's a couple people beginning a conversation-- intervene. If it's one person posting things steadily, hit em with a DM: "to preserve the server purpose, there's no room built for these posts — hope that's not annoying to hear."

Feel free to add a personal note if one wishes: "honestly though I'd love to see more cat pics, are we in any other mutual servers?" or "can you instead please just funnel these to my DMs? thank you in advance lol"

## Someone asking why a topic isn't allowed

- "Nothing's wrong with the topic, this server's just not designed to support it. The server does only one thing, on purpose."

## Witnessing a conversation going sideways

- "Let's drop it here. If either of you want to talk it through with me, feel free to DM me."
- "Let's put a pin in this. It'll be good to take some time, and come back to it after a day or so, maybe in a private DM or group chat."

## Removal, general

- "Removing you from the server. Not looking to argue about it, and I'm not going to make a post about it either."

## If someone is in forreal-forreal crisis

This is rare to see in a detectable format online, but super worth it to have a plan ready just in case. Signs that this is a forreal-forreal: talk of not being around, of having a plan, of things being unbearable right now rather than lately; talk from someone who *never* opens up in a public forum about stuff like this. Take that seriously.

**What to do:** Don't start with moderation, just ask how they're doing. Stay with it long enough to get a better idea of where they're at mentally, and until you get a chance to point them somewhere genuinely helpful.

- "That sounds really heavy. Are you safe right now?"
- "I'm not the right person to carry this, but I don't want to just leave you with it either. Can I help you find someone to talk to that can offer you the right support?"

## Appendix: server rules

- **18+ only.** Everyone here is an adult, and it's easier to relax when that's a given.
- **Discord's Community Guidelines apply,** across all of Discord. They cover the serious things, so there's no need to repeat them here.
- **Keep images and clips to game content.** There's no age-restricted channel here, so there's nowhere for anything sexual or graphic to live — that includes in-game footage.
- **Channels are for the game they're named for.** Not because the rest of life doesn't matter — there's just nowhere here built to hold it.
- **Spoilers are a shared job.** In text chat, use tags. In voice, ask before you get into something, or say you're not caught up. Nothing is too old to be worth a quick check — just ask the people active in the conversation.
- **Something wrong?** Use `/report`, the button below, or the form. Anonymous unless you choose otherwise.
